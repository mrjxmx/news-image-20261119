Shenzhen · APEC Summit

A FIRST: TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN SIT DOWN TOGETHER

The US, Chinese and Russian presidents held the first trilateral meeting in history, at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC. In 90 minutes on Ukraine, Iran, nuclear arms, trade and AI, they pledged to keep talking and reaffirmed that a nuclear war must never be fought — but announced no breakthrough.

Entertainment · Launch Day

GTA VI IS OUT — AND IT IS ENORMOUS

After a 13-year wait, Rockstar’s game launched at midnight around the world, New Zealand first, on PS5 and Xbox. Sensor Tower estimates 19 million copies and $1.84bn in sales already — a record. Critics scored it 99 on Metacritic; 7.9 million watched live streams at once.

Ukraine War

UKRAINE HITS THE KERCH BRIDGE A THIRD TIME

Neptune missiles struck the bridge’s railway span overnight — the third attack in four days, after the road arch collapsed. Trains to Crimea halted for hours. In Shenzhen, Putin vowed: “We will respond.”

Middle East

GULF ON EDGE: TANKER HIT NEAR HORMUZ

A tanker was struck near Khasab, Oman, and Saudi defences intercepted a Houthi cruise missile, a day after Iran fired drones at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain. The Pentagon put the war’s cost above $48bn; Washington weighed fresh strikes.
